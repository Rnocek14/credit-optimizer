## Optimization Strategy

### Why "Cheapest"?
1. **Sophia first**: $99/month for unlimited courses = best value for gen-ed
2. **CLEP for variety**: $93/exam for subjects Sophia doesn't offer well
3. **DSST for business**: $100/exam for upper-level business credits
4. **Study.com sparingly**: Only for upper-level courses not available via exams
5. **TESU minimal**: Only what's required for residency (15 credits)

### Alternative "Fastest" Strategy (Not This Template)
- Use Study.com for everything (4 months total)
- Skip exams entirely
- Cost: ~$4,500, Time: 4-6 months

### Alternative "Standard" Strategy (Not This Template)
- Mix of community college + TESU
- More traditional approach
- Cost: ~$15,000-20,000, Time: 4 years
