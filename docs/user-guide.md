# EduTree V4 User Guide

## Getting Started

EduTree V4 is an interactive degree planning tool that helps you visualize your path to graduation with real-time policy compliance checking.

## Understanding Your Plan View

### Year Nodes
- **Spine nodes** represent each academic year (Year 1-4)
- Shows total credits planned for that year
- Displays load health: Underloaded (<24), Balanced (24-36), or Overloaded (>36)

### Course Nodes
- **Color-coded by category**: Blue (Core CS), Green (Math), Gray (Gen Ed), Yellow (Elective), Purple (Capstone)
- **Semester badges**: 🍂 Fall or 🌸 Spring
- **Status indicators**: Completed (green), In Progress (blue), Planned (amber)

### Module Cards
- Group related courses by requirement (e.g., CS Foundations, Humanities)
- Show progress bars and completion status
- Click "Browse Options" to explore marketplace alternatives
- Expand/collapse to manage screen space

## Badge Meanings

### Category Badges
- **💻 Core**: Core computer science courses
- **📐 Math**: Math and science requirements
- **📚 Gen Ed**: General education courses
- **🎯 Elective**: Elective courses
- **🎓 Capstone**: Capstone project courses
- **🔁 Transfer**: Transfer credit from another institution

### Policy Badges
- **✅ Articulated**: Guaranteed transfer via Florida articulation agreement
- **⚠️ Non-Transfer**: Course does not count toward 60-credit transfer limit
- **🛒 Marketplace**: Alternative provider selected from marketplace

## Using the Marketplace

1. Click "Browse Options" on any module card
2. Review available courses from various providers (Coursera, edX, etc.)
3. Filter by difficulty, duration, and skills
4. Select a course to add it to your plan
5. System automatically checks policy compliance

## Understanding Compliance

### Compliance Score Panel
- **Overall Score**: Percentage of policy requirements met
- **Transfer Limit**: Max 60 credits from transfer sources
- **Residency**: Minimum 30 credits must be completed at your institution
- **Errors**: Must be resolved before plan is valid
- **Warnings**: Recommendations for improvement

### Degree Progress Panel
- **Total Credits**: Your progress toward 120 credit requirement
- **By Category**: Credits completed in each requirement area
- **Missing Courses**: Specific courses still needed

## Tips for Success

1. **Check Compliance Early**: Review policy violations before finalizing your plan
2. **Use Articulation**: Look for "✅ Articulated" badges to ensure smooth transfers
3. **Balance Your Load**: Aim for 30 credits/year for on-time graduation
4. **Explore Alternatives**: Marketplace offers flexible options (CLEP, online courses)
5. **Export Regularly**: Save PDF snapshots to track changes over time

## Exporting Your Plan

1. Click "Export PDF" in Compliance Panel
2. Enter your name
3. PDF includes:
   - Compliance summary
   - Module breakdown
   - Policy violations
   - Articulation mappings

## Sharing Your Plan

1. Click "Share Plan" in Degree Progress Panel
2. Copy the generated link
3. Share with advisors or colleagues
4. They can view (but not edit) your plan snapshot

## When to Consult Your Advisor

- Red "Error" badges appear
- Transfer limit warnings
- Expired credits in your plan
- Missing critical prerequisites
- Planning to take courses at non-articulated institutions

## Keyboard Shortcuts

- **Zoom In/Out**: Mouse wheel or trackpad pinch
- **Pan**: Click and drag canvas
- **Fit View**: Double-click canvas background

## Troubleshooting

**Module cards overlapping?**
- Use Module Toolbar to collapse all, then expand one at a time

**Can't find a course?**
- Check if it's hidden in a collapsed module
- Use "Browse Options" to search marketplace

**Policy warning unclear?**
- Hover over badges for tooltips (coming soon)
- Export PDF for detailed policy explanations

## Support

For technical issues or policy questions, contact your academic advisor or visit the help center.
